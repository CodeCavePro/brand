CODECAVE is dark, round, and violet — in that order of importance.

CODECAVE is a software delivery studio. Its public surface is a marketing and lead-generation site whose entire job is to turn a visitor into one booked consultation. Six services are offered — Cloud & DevOps, E-Commerce, Autodesk plugins, Automation & AI, HubSpot, and AR & VR — and each is titled by the outcome it produces rather than the technology it uses. The stack is Astro 7, Vue 3 and Tailwind 4, with GSAP for entrance motion, Lenis for smooth scroll and Strapi as the content backend.
