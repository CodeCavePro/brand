The reserved cyan

Inspect this and confirm it never appears as a UI color anywhere else in the package.

Cyan --color-brand-400 · #20EFFE

Reserved. #20EFFE appears only inside gradient and orb motifs — the technology card blends brand-500 → brand-400 → brand-500 at low alpha (homepage/technology-card.vue). Never a CTA, body color, large field, or photographic grade. It is a light effect, not a UI color.
