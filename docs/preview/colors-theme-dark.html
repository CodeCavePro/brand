The dark canvas — CODECAVE’s only theme

The defining decision of the system: the page and its cards sit one barely-perceptible step apart. Depth is carried by radius, border and the upward glow — never by lightening a surface. Check the four steps below read as separate without reading as contrasted.

The four surface steps

Total luminance spread across all four is smaller than a single step in most dark UI kits. That is intentional and must survive review.

--color-surface-primary · #050505 · the page
--color-surface-secondary · #0D0D0F · card fill (one step off the page)
--color-surface-tertiary · #141319 · raised / hovered card, icon tiles
--color-surface-primary-hover · #2A2637 · surface hover
--color-surface-quaternary · #2E2C33 · borders, dividers, checkbox outline
