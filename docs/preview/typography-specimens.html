Typography

Satoshi, one family, nine steps

Display and body are the same face. That single-family austerity is the brand’s own decision, not an oversight. This card renders the real cuts shipped in fonts/ — if the specimens below look like a system sans, the @font-face binding in colors_and_type.css has broken.

Family and fallbacks

Bound in colors_and_type.css from fonts/Satoshi-*.woff2 with .woff fallbacks. The mono stack is used only for token readouts and code, never for brand copy.

--font-sans Satoshi, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", "Noto Sans", Arial, sans-serif
--font-mono ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", "Courier New", monospace

Production uses only 400 and 700. The package binds real Light, Medium and Black cuts so nothing is faux-weighted, but designs should stay on 400/700 to match the shipped site.

The eyebrow — the one signature move

A bold violet line stacked with a larger light lead line, opening nearly every section. Source: services/heading.astro, homepage/expertise.astro. It is one sentence broken across two colors — never two sentences. This is the system’s only typographic flourish; do not add a second.

Our expertise is part of their success

We build software. We also do design.

off-system — two separate sentences, order reversed
