Spacing · card 2 of 3

Radius — the loudest brand signal

Corners do more brand work here than the violet does. A 24px card is the default, not a soft option; section panels reach 120px. Nothing in this system has a small corner, and that single constraint is the fastest way to tell an on-brand surface from a generic one.

The eight steps, at true size

--radius-md · 12px
